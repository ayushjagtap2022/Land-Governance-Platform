1. One-Line Framing You are not building a dashboard. You are building a national research-and-policy ecosystem — a repository + AI search + GIS + analytics + policy simulation + collaboration + innovation portal, unified under role-based access, for researchers, government officials, academic institutions, and the public.

6. USPs — How to Stand Out

=== PAGE 6 ===
1. Policy Simulation as the centerpiece, not a checkbox feature. Almost every competing team will build dashboards; almost none will build a working "what-if" simulator. Lead your demo with this. 

2. Explainable AI everywhere. Every AI answer or recommendation shows its source document. For a government audience, traceability beats raw AI flash. 

3. Multilingual public access. A Hindi/regional-language search layer for public users is rare and directly serves the PS's stated public-access requirement. 

4. Built for integration, not isolation. A working API demo + architecture diagram showing how this slots into existing DoLR/state systems signals platform-scale thinking, not hackathon-toy thinking. 

5. Innovation Portal as a living ecosystem.
